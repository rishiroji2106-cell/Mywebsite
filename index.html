🌸 Where It Started

Some stories don't begin with love at first sight. Sometimes they begin with family, childhood memories, teasing, little fights, and two people who slowly become important to each other.

Pavan Kumar was born on 27 September 2000.

He studied at school, completed his diploma at Visakha College, and later studied engineering at Avanthi Engineering College.

He was placed in Guntur as a Senior Electrician Engineer.

I had known Pavan through our family. We met at family functions and parties.

During those days, we were basically Tom and Jerry. 🐱🐭

We teased each other, argued, and shared our own funny little moments.

When I was around 10 years old, I already liked him as a person.

💬 The First Conversations

Around 2019, our conversations slowly started.

In December, I sent him my first message.

It wasn't a dramatic beginning. It was simply one message that slowly turned into more conversations.

💔 My 10th-Class Chapter

When I was in 10th class, I experienced a one-sided love that didn't work out.

I told Pavan about it.

He supported me when I was feeling low and encouraged me not to keep crying.

He was there when I was down, and that support stayed with me.

After some time, we stopped talking for a while.

Later, we started chatting again.

Somewhere along the way, I realized that my feelings for him were still there.

I slowly developed a crush on him.

💔 The Girlfriend Chapter

Later, Pavan told me that he had a girlfriend.

I felt disappointed, so I decided to focus on my studies.

But our story didn't end there.

During my 1st year of engineering, we started talking much more.

Our conversations became longer and more frequent.

And as we became closer, we also had more fights and misunderstandings.

There were both good days and difficult days.

🌧️ When He Was Hurting

Later, Pavan and his girlfriend had problems, and eventually they broke up.

During that difficult time, I supported him.

He had once supported me when I was hurting, and now I wanted to be there for him.

🌱 We Both Changed

As time passed, I changed in some ways because I wanted to become the person he wanted me to be.

Pavan was changing too.

He became more focused on his work and career.

Somewhere during this journey, his feelings for me started to grow.

From August onward, I noticed him showing his feelings toward me more openly.

🎂 September 2025 — A Special Birthday Memory

Pavan's birthday is September 27.

In September 2025, from September 1 to September 26, I gave him advance birthday wishes.

During that time, I gave him my first gift. 🎁

We also had a picture together that day. 📸

It became one of those memories I wanted to keep forever.

📍 November 4 — He Came to Meet Me

On November 4, Pavan came to Simhachalam to meet me.

It was another special moment in our growing story.

Around this period, we were also talking every day.

We shared even the smallest information about our days with each other.

Those little conversations became an important part of our connection.

🎂 My Birthday

My birthday is in December.

By then, our everyday conversations and growing connection had made the little moments between us special.

🪁 Four Years of Sankranthi Memories

For the past four years, Pavan has come to meet me during Sankranthi.

Sankranthi 2025 was especially memorable.

In December, we did online shopping together for Sankranthi. 🛍️

Then in January, we went for offline shopping together.

We also went on an Araku trip, where we created many special memories together.

On Bhogi day, we went shopping too.

There was even a tiny moment when I touched his hand, and it felt incredibly special to me. 🤍

🌸 January 16, 2026

January 16, 2026 became another memorable day and a first special affectionate moment for us.

It was one of those little moments that stayed in my heart.

🌹 February — Love, Fights & Ishq

February brought a mixture of emotions.

There were fights, love, misunderstandings, and ishq.

It wasn't always perfect, but every emotion became another part of our story.

🌸 March — Little Signs of Love

In March, we started keeping loving statuses.

On March 19, 2026, we had matching dresses. 👗❤️

It was a simple moment, but it became another beautiful memory.

Then came March 27, 2026.

Pavan suddenly had an accident.

I was shocked and extremely worried about him.

That moment reminded me how much his presence mattered to me.

🏙️ Three Months in Vizag

After the accident, Pavan stayed in Vizag for about three months.

Those three months became especially meaningful.

We spent more time together and created many memories.

🛕 May 19 — Simhachalam

On May 19, 2026, we went to Simhachalam Temple together.

❤️ July — More Love & Care

In July 2026, I noticed more loving statuses, more memories, and more expressions of love and care.

Our connection felt stronger.

🏠 August — A Week Together

In August 2026, Pavan came to Vizag for his housewarming.

For about a week, he would pick me up and drop me back at my house.
